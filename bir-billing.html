Cloud Soaring at Billing & Tibetan Serenity of Bir Colony

Framed by the dramatic granite ramparts of the Dhauladhar mountains in the Kangra highlands, Bir Billing is world-renowned as India's paragliding mecca and the second highest paragliding takeoff site on Earth. Billing's high alpine ridge at 2,400 meters offers world-class thermal aerodynamics, while the Tibetan refugee settlement of Bir below cradles centuries-old Buddhist monasteries, organic tea gardens, and peaceful eco-living retreats.

Monasteries, Tea Gardens & Landing Point

Peaceful Buddhist gompas, British-era tea estates, and the world's most vibrant glider landing zone.

1. Chokling Monastery

Pema Ewam Chögar Gyurme Ling

Situated in the heart of Bir Tibetan Colony. Features an imposing white stupa, a grand golden Padmasambhava statue, and vibrant prayer halls where visitors can meditate and witness evening Tibetan prayers.

Location: Tibetan Colony Grand Golden Stupa

2. Palpung Sherabling Monastery

Seat of the 12th Tai Situ Rinpoche

Tucked inside the dense Bhattu pine forest, this monumental monastery complex houses a Grammy-winning Buddhist chant choir, a towering gilded Maitreya Buddha statue, and quiet meditative forest trails.

Distance: 6 km from Bir Forest Buddhist Sanctuary

3. Bir Tea Factory & Estates

Sprawling British-Era Tea Bushes

Bir is surrounded by manicured organic tea plantations sloping gently toward the Kangra plains. Tour the working cooperative tea factory to witness traditional leaf drying and purchase fragrant first-flush orthodox tea.

Activity: Cycling & Tea Tasting GI-Tagged Kangra Brews

4. Bir Landing Site (Sunset Point)

Glider Touchdowns & Golden Hour Hangout

The social heart of Bir. Every evening hundreds of travelers gather on the green meadows to watch colorful paragliders perform graceful spirals and touch down safely against glowing sunset hues over the hills.

Vibe: Sunset Music & Street Snacks Glider Touchdown Arena

5. Dharmalaya Institute

Sustainable Living & Earthen Architecture

An eco-campus dedicated to sustainable living, organic farming, and compassionate lifestyle. Built entirely of earthen adobe bricks, bamboo, and slate, it offers yoga, meditation, and green architecture courses.

Setting: Quiet Hillside Woods Mud & Bamboo Retreat

6. Deer Park Institute

Classical Indian & Tibetan Philosophy

Established under the patronage of the Dalai Lama to re-awaken classical Indian wisdom traditions. Features tranquil gardens, an extensive Buddhist library, art workshops, and mindfulness seminars.

Location: Tibetan Colony Mindfulness Seminars

Wonders of Billing Ridge, Baijnath & Waterfalls

World-class paragliding launches, 13th-century stone jyotirlingas, and hidden jungle streams.

1. Billing Takeoff Ridge

World's #2 Paragliding Launchpad

Located 14 km winding uphill through oak woods from Bir. The vast grassy ridge drops precipitously towards the valley, creating exceptional thermal lift that allows paragliders to soar for hours alongside eagles.

Drive: 45 mins from Bir 360° Alpine Panoramas

2. Bangoru Waterfall

Hidden Forest Stream & Plunge Pool

A secluded waterfall tucked inside a boulder-strewn jungle gorge just 4 km from Bir. A short 20-minute walk through terraced fields and shady pines brings you to this natural rock pool ideal for a refreshing dip.

Hike: 20 min easy walk Crystal Clear Pool

3. Baijnath Shiva Temple

13th-Century Nagara Jyotirlinga Shrine

Situated 12 km from Bir on the banks of River Binwa. This magnificent stone temple built in 1204 AD by two merchant brothers houses the revered Vaidyanath ('Lord of Physicians') Jyotirlinga, untouched by time.

Architecture: Ancient Nagara Stone Sacred Jyotirlinga

4. Gunehar Village & Stream Walk

Cobblestone Art Village & River Pools

A peaceful Gaddi tribal village located just 2.5 km from Bir. Known for hosting the 4Cs Art Festival with vibrant building murals, outdoor art installations, and a quiet mountain river trail leading to hidden bathing pools.

Distance: 2.5 km from Bir Street Murals & River Walks

5. Rajgundha Valley Trek

Pristine 360° Alpine River Meadow

A breathtaking 14 km ridge trek starting from Billing winding through dense oak and rhododendron forests to the isolated traditional valley of Rajgundha. Offers crystal streams and stargazing camp spots.

Trail: 4 - 5 Hours from Billing Wild Stargazing Camps

6. Barot Valley Day Trip

Uhl River Gorges & Trout Angling

Situated 50 km from Bir along the raging Uhl River. Renowned for its British-built 1920s funicular trolley railway, trout breeding farms, and dense deodar forests ideal for peaceful riverside camping.

Drive: 2 Hours from Bir Trout Hatchery & River Camp
Handcrafted Journey

The Ideal 3-Day Bir Billing Flight & Zen Tour

Engineered for sky adventure, Tibetan spirituality, and organic cafe relaxation.

DAY 1

Tibetan Colony Cycling, Chokling Gompa & Sunset at Landing Site

Arrive in Bir and check into your eco-lodge or guesthouse. Rent a mountain bicycle to explore the peaceful lanes of the Tibetan Colony. Visit Chokling Monastery to admire its grand gilded stupa and spin the brass prayer wheels. In the late afternoon, head to the wide green Landing Site to sip spiced ginger tea while watching paragliders float down out of the crimson evening sky.

Tibetan Colony Bicycle Tour Chokling Golden Stupa Landing Site Sunset Vibe
DAY 2

Tandem Paragliding from Billing & Bangoru Waterfall Hike

Drive 14 km up to the 2,400m high grassy ridge of Billing. Strap in with your certified pilot and launch into the sky for a thrilling 20 to 30-minute tandem flight soaring high over valleys, pine forests, and tea terraces before touching down in Bir. In the afternoon, take a relaxing 20-minute jungle walk to Bangoru Waterfall to unwind and dip your feet in crystalline rock pools.

On your return route, stop at the 1204 AD stone Baijnath Temple to receive blessings at the ancient Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga.

Aviation & Safety

Guidelines for Bir Billing Paragliding Trips

  • ✓ Pilot Certification: Fly strictly with certified tandem pilots registered with the Himachal Tourism Department. Always ensure they carry a reserve parachute.
  • ✓ Thermal Wind Timings: Flight operations depend completely on air thermals. Flying is usually conducted between 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM; flights stop immediately during strong gusts.
  • ✓ Altitude Dressing: Billing takeoff is at 2,400 meters and winds can be brisk. Wear sturdy sports shoes and a windbreaker jacket during your flight.
