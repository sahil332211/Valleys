Roaring Waters of Manali & Snowy Glades of Solang Valley

Nestled under the majestic Pir Panjal and Dhauladhar ranges along the glacial Beas River, Manali is Himachal's premier mountain paradise. From the tranquil 16th-century wooden pagodas and bohemian Old Manali lanes to the high-adrenaline snow arenas of Solang Valley and the marvel of the Atal Tunnel, this valley offers an intoxicating blend of heritage and alpine thrill.

Must-Visit Sights in Manali

Ancient wooden temples, sacred thermal springs, and vibrant bohemian enclaves.

1. Hadimba Devi Temple

Four-Tiered Pagoda in Cedar Woods

Dedicated to Hadimba, wife of Pandava prince Bhima. Built by Raja Bahadur Singh, this exquisite wooden pagoda features intricate mythological carvings and deer antlers beneath giant deodars.

Location: Dhungri Forest (1.5 km) Heritage Pagoda Sanctuary

2. Old Manali & Manu Temple

Traditional Kathkuni Homes & Cafes

Cross the Manalsu bridge to enter an older world of slate roofs, apple orchards, and the historic Manu Temple—the only shrine in India dedicated to Sage Manu. Famous for artisanal bakeries and live music.

Vibe: Artsy, Relaxed, Scenic Manu Maharishi Temple

3. Jogini Waterfalls

Sacred Cascade Hike via Vashisht

A scenic 45-minute trek starting from Vashisht village through pine woods and apple groves. The pristine waterfall tumbles down multi-tiered rock faces with views of the snow-clad Rohtang peak.

Hike Duration: 1 - 1.5 Hours Apple Grove Trail

4. Vashisht Hot Springs & Temple

4,000-Year-Old Sacred Village

Perched on the cliffside of the Beas River, this ancient village is named after Sage Vashistha. Its natural geothermal hot water baths possess therapeutic minerals said to heal skin and muscle ailments.

Distance: 3 km from Mall Road Therapeutic Thermal Dip

5. Mall Road & Tibetan Gompas

Gadhan Thekchhokling Gompa

The bustling heart of town lined with woolen shawls, wooden handicrafts, and Himachali sweets. Behind the avenue stands the peaceful 1960 Tibetan monastery with vibrant thangkas and prayer wheels.

Shopping: Kullu Shawls & Honey Tibetan Thangka Art

6. Van Vihar Nature Park

Sky-Touching Deodars by Beas

Located directly off the Mall Road, this serene forest of centenarian deodars extends down to the stony banks of the Beas River. Features a small boating pond and shaded walking trails.

Activity: Evening Strolls & Boating Peaceful Riverfront

Wonders of Solang Valley & High Passes

Snow sports amphitheaters, engineering marvels, and glacial passes of the Pir Panjal.

1. Solang Adventure Arena

Skiing, Zorbing & Paragliding Hub

A high bowl surrounded by towering glaciers. In winter it transforms into India's premier skiing destination with ropeways; in summer, lush meadows host tandem paragliding, ATV rides, and zorbing.

Distance: 14 km from Manali Ropeway & Ski Slopes

2. Atal Tunnel & Sissu Waterfall

Gateway to Lahaul Valley

The world's longest highway tunnel above 10,000 feet, boring straight through the Pir Panjal. Emerge at the North Portal into Lahaul to witness the roaring 50m Sissu Waterfall and willow groves.

Drive Time: 40 mins from Solang Engineering Triumph

3. Anjani Mahadev

The "Amarnath of Himachal"

A 2 km trek from Solang leads to this sacred cliff where mother Anjani meditated. In peak winter, the cliff waterfall freezes solid into a colossal 30-40 foot natural ice Shiva lingam.

Hike: 45 min walk or horseback Natural Frozen Ice Spire

4. Rohtang Pass

The Crown Gateway to Leh-Ladakh

The iconic high mountain pass connecting Kullu to the stark Chandra and Spiti valleys. Offers jaw-dropping 360-degree vistas of perennial glaciers, craggy precipices, and pristine snow fields.

Permit: NGT Permit Required Glacier Snowfields

5. Gulaba & Kothi Gorge

Deep Pine Glades & Snow Strolls

Charming pastoral hamlets en route to Rohtang. Kothi is famous for its dramatic deep gorge carved by the Beas River, while Gulaba’s meadows are the filming backdrop of iconic Bollywood snow scenes.

Distance: 15 km on Rohtang Road Bollywood Landscape

6. Hampta Pass Trail & Sethan

Buddhist Igloo Village & Crossover

Rising above Prini village, the curvy road leads to Sethan (an authentic Buddhist village famous for winter igloo stays) and the trailhead of the dramatic Hampta Pass trek bridging green Kullu to desert Spiti.

Activity: Igloo Stays & Bouldering Crossover Trek Route

The Ideal 3-Day Manali & Solang Itinerary

Expertly crafted for adrenaline, peaceful heritage, and cozy mountain evenings.

DAY 1

Arrival, Hadimba Sanctuary & Old Manali Bohemian Trail

Arrive in Manali and settle into your pine-facing hotel or cottage. Begin your afternoon with a walk through the deodars of Dhungri to seek blessings at the 1553 AD Hadimba Pagoda. Cross over the river bridge into Old Manali to explore Sage Manu's shrine, handmade craft stalls, and chill at a riverside cafe enjoying fresh trout and live guitar strums.

Hadimba Pagoda Temple Old Manali Cafes Van Vihar Evening Walk
DAY 2

Solang Valley Adventure & The Atal Tunnel Expedition

Set out early for Solang Valley. Take the scenic cable car ropeway, try tandem paragliding, or snow tubing on the alpine glades. Continue driving north through the marvel of the 9.02 km Atal Tunnel to emerge into the dramatic barren mountains of Lahaul. Stop at Sissu to behold its thundering glacial waterfall before returning to Manali.

Solang Paragliding / Skiing Atal Tunnel North Portal Sissu Glacial Waterfall
DAY 3

Jogini Waterfall Trek, Vashisht Sulphur Bath & Mall Road

Drive to Vashisht village. Take the exhilarating 45-minute trek through apple orchards up to the multi-tier Jogini Waterfalls. Return to dip your tired feet into the sacred thermal sulphur kunds of Vashisht. Spend your final evening strolling Mall Road, picking up authentic Kullu handloom shawls, organic wild honey, and savoring hot Himachali Siddu.

Authentic mountain dishes and bohemian culinary treasures.

🐟

Pan-Fried River Trout

🥧

Warm Apple Crumble

Made from freshly harvested Kullu valley apples, baked with cinnamon, brown sugar, and served with vanilla ice cream in Old Manali.

Travel Smart

Guidelines for Manali & Solang Trips

  • ✓ Rohtang Permits: Visiting Rohtang Pass strictly requires an online National Green Tribunal (NGT) permit. Book 2-3 days in advance.
  • ✓ Atal Tunnel Safety: Speed limits inside the 9km tunnel are strictly monitored (max 60 km/h). Stopping or overtaking inside is strictly prohibited.
  • ✓ Winter Snow Gear: In winter, hire 4x4 vehicles with tire chains for driving up beyond Solang Valley or toward Sethan to prevent skidding.
