The Complete Catalog

Famous Places in Himachal Pradesh

From lush pine sanctuaries and high cold deserts to spiritual river valleys. Filter by mood, search by district, and track your lifetime memories.

Dalhousie

Victorian Serenity & Pine Aromatherapy

Spread across five forested hills. Celebrated for Victorian architecture, tranquil Bakrota strolls, the high singing winds of Dainkund Peak, and Subhash Baoli natural spring.

Key Highlights
Dainkund Peak Subhash Baoli St. John Church Bakrota Round
Best: Mar - Jun & Dec - Feb Peaceful Vibe

Khajjiar

The Mini Switzerland of India

A breathtaking saucer meadow enclosed by dense deodar forests. Features a sacred central lake with a floating island, ancient 12th-c Khajji Nag wooden temple, and Kalatop sanctuary trails.

Key Highlights
Khajjiar Lake Kalatop Wildlife Khajji Nag Temple Zorbing Glade
Best: All Year (Green / Snow) Alpine Meadow Vibe

Manali & Solang

Valley of Gods & Alpine Thrills

Nestled on the Beas River, Manali is Himachal's premier hub for adventure sports, snow activities in Solang Valley, the engineering marvel Atal Tunnel, and Old Manali's bohemian cafes.

Key Highlights
Solang Valley Atal Tunnel Hadimba Temple Jogini Falls
Best: Oct - Jun Snow & Adventure

Dharamshala & McLeodganj

Tibetan Spirituality & Dhauladhar Ridges

Known as Little Lhasa, residence of the 14th Dalai Lama. Fluttering Tibetan prayer flags, Tsuglagkhang monastery complex, sacred Bhagsunag falls, and the famous Triund ridge trek.

Key Highlights
Dalai Lama Temple Triund Trek Bhagsu Waterfall Norbulingka
Best: Sep - Jun Spiritual & Trekking

Spiti Valley

The Mystical Cold Desert of Monasteries

Raw, barren geological grandeur sandwiched between Tibet and India. Home to the thousand-year-old cliffside Key Monastery, high-altitude Chandratal Lake, and crystal clear night skies.

Key Highlights
Key Gompa Chandratal Lake Komic Post Office Dhankar Monastery
Best: Jun - Oct High Altitude Expedition

Shimla & Kufri

Queen of Hills & Heritage Toy Train

The historic summer capital of British India. Famous for the UNESCO-inscribed Kalka-Shimla mountain railway, vibrant Ridge promenade, neo-Gothic Christ Church, and snowy pine slopes of Kufri.

Key Highlights
The Ridge Mall Road Jakhoo Temple Toy Train
Best: Year-round Heritage & Family

Kasol & Parvati Valley

Hippie Trails & Rushing River Magic

Flanked by the raging Parvati River and dense coniferous woods. Gateway to the Kheerganga hot water sulfur springs trek, Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, and quaint villages like Tosh and Chalal.

Key Highlights
Kheerganga Trek Manikaran Sahib Chalal Bridge Walk Tosh Waterfall
Best: Apr - Jun & Sep - Nov Bohemian Riverside

Bir Billing

World's 2nd Highest Paragliding Take-Off

Take off from Billing at 2,400m and float gracefully above emerald tea gardens to land in Bir. Famous for Tibetan eco-villages, monasteries, cycling routes, and mind-blowing sunsets.

Key Highlights
Tandem Flight Chokling Gompa Billing Sunset Point Bir Tea Factory
Best: Oct - Nov & Mar - Jun Aerial Thrill

Jibhi & Tirthan Valley

Untouched Cedar Hideaways & Trout Streams

An unspoiled hidden gem with Victorian-style wooden cottages, gushing crystal streams, the mysterious Serolsar Lake hike via Jalori Pass, and the UNESCO Great Himalayan National Park.

Key Highlights
Jibhi Waterfalls Jalori Pass (3,120m) Serolsar Lake Chehni Kothi
Best: Mar - Jun & Sep - Nov Offbeat Calm
