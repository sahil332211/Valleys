Cold Desert Majesty of Spiti Valley & Celestial Silence of Chandratal

Spiti, literally meaning "The Middle Land", is a mystical cold desert mountain wonderland isolated from the world by massive Himalayan walls. Perched along the braided turquoise Spiti River, this high plateau cradles thousand-year-old cliffside Buddhist gompas, ancient mud citadels, moon-like geological gorges, the world's highest inhabited settlements, and pristine starry skies of cosmic clarity.

Legendary Gompas & Heritage Fortresses

Millennia-old monastic citadels, clay murals, and spiritual seats perched on sheer cliffs.

1. Key Gompa (Kye Monastery)

11th-Century Fortress Monastery

The biggest and oldest monastery in Spiti. Built as a Pasada fortress on a conical rock hill, it houses precious thangkas, ancient manuscripts, and sacred musical trumpets. Serves delicious herbal tea to all visitors.

Distance: 14 km from Kaza Gelugpa Seat & Ancient Murals

2. Dhankar Monastery & Fort

Cliffhanger Castle over River Confluence

Literally meaning "Fort on a Cliff". Precariously perched on the edge of a razor-sharp 300m cliff overlooking the confluence of the Spiti and Pin rivers. Once the ancient capital of the rulers of Spiti.

Hike: 1 hr trek to Dhankar Lake Dramatic Cliff Architecture

3. Tabo Monastery

The "Ajanta of the Himalayas"

Over 1,030 years old, this mud-brick UNESCO World Heritage candidate preserves the purest Indo-Tibetan clay statues, life-sized bodhisattvas, and breathtaking 10th-century wall frescoes in 9 prayer temples.

Location: Tabo Village (48 km) 1,000+ Year Mud Frescoes

4. Kaza & Spiti Riverbed

Commercial & Cultural Epicenter

Divided into Kaza Khas (old town) and Kaza Soma (new township). Bustles with traveler cafes, handmade wool and yak cheese shops, Tibetan bakeries, and the world's highest retail fuel petrol pump.

Vibe: Traveler Hub & River Cafes Highest Petrol Station

5. Kungri Monastery (Pin Valley)

Seat of the Tantric Nyingmapa Order

The second oldest monastery in Spiti and the only one belonging to the Nyingmapa sect. Famous for dramatic sword dances performed during the annual Buchen festival in the dramatic red Pin Valley.

Location: Pin Valley Tantric Buddhist Traditions

6. Tangyud Monastery

Among the World's Highest Gompas

Belonging to the Sakya school, this fortified castle-like monastery stands sentinel over the extreme high-altitude village of Komic. Its central sanctum holds ancient stuffed wild animals and protective deities.

Location: Komic Village Extreme Altitude Sanctum

Wonders of Chandratal, Kunzum & High Villages

Turquoise glacial waters, celestial mountain passes, and the highest inhabited outposts on Earth.

1. Chandratal (The Moon Lake)

Crescent Turquoise Alpine Jewel

Named for its crescent moon shape, this pristine Ramsar wetland lake shifts colors from emerald green to deep cobalt blue as sunlight dances on the surrounding scree slopes of the Chandra Bhaga range.

Trail: 1 km walk from parking Chandra Bhaga Panoramas

2. Kunzum Pass & Goddess Temple

Gateway Between Lahaul & Spiti

One of India's highest motorable passes. Travelers stop to complete a sacred clockwise circumambulation around the Kunzum Mata stupa. Offers awe-inspiring vistas of Bara-Shigri, the 2nd longest glacier in the Himalayas.

Season: Jun to Oct only Bara-Shigri Glacier Views

3. Hikkim Post Office

World's Highest Operational Post Office

Functioning since 1983 in a traditional stone and mud house, postal staff still hand-stamp and dispatch postcards sent by travelers across the world via foot couriers down to Kaza.

Activity: Send stamped postcards PIN Code: 172114

4. Komic Village

World's Highest Motorable Settlement

Meaning "Eye of a Snow Cock", this tiny whitewashed village with barely 150 residents is the highest village in the world connected by motorable road. Features the high-altitude Spiti Organic Farm Cafe.

Distance: 18 km from Kaza Highest Motorable Road

5. Langza & Golden Buddha

Fossil Village of the Tethys Sea

Dominated by a colossal 1,000-year-old golden Buddha statue gazing towards the sacred snow pyramid of Chau Chau Kang Nilda. The village soil is rich in prehistoric marine ammonite fossils from the ancient Tethys Sea.

View: Chau Chau Kang Nilda (6,303m) Prehistoric Marine Fossils

6. Mudh Village & Pin Valley

Snow Leopard & Ibex Territory

The last motorable outpost of the dramatic Pin Valley National Park. Surrounded by striking pink, green, and purple geological mountains, Mudh is the trailhead for the treacherous Bhaba and Pin-Parvati high pass treks.

Fauna: Snow Leopard & Siberian Ibex Pink Mineral Mountains

The Ideal 3-Day Spiti Highlands Circuit

The premier central Kaza loop covering high gompas, highest post office, and lunar lakes.

DAY 1

Kaza Base Arrival, Acclimatization & Key Gompa Twilight

Arrive in Kaza (ensure gradual acclimatization if driving via Manali or Kinnaur). Rest during the morning and sip warm garlic soup. In the afternoon, drive 14 km along the Spiti riverbed to Key Monastery. Climb through the centuries-old mud stairs, spin ancient prayer wheels, and enjoy salted butter tea with monks while taking in the sunset over the valley.

Key Gompa Sunset Garlic Soup Acclimatization Kaza Market Cafes
DAY 2

The World's Highest Circuit: Langza, Komic & Hikkim Post Office

Ascend to Langza to view the majestic golden Buddha overlooking the 6,303m Chau Chau Kang Nilda peak. Search for ancient marine fossils along the scree. Drive to Komic (4,587m) to visit Tangyud Monastery, and end at Hikkim (4,400m) to hand-post written postcards from the highest post office on planet Earth.

Langza Golden Buddha Hikkim Postcard Dispatch Komic Highest Road
DAY 3

Dhankar Cliff Fortress, Pin Valley & Tabo 1,000-Year Mud Sanctum

Head downstream to Dhankar cliff monastery perched high over the confluence of the Spiti and Pin rivers. Hike up to Dhankar Lake if energy permits. Drive into the crimson canyons of Pin Valley to explore Kungri Monastery, then reach Tabo to marvel at the 996 AD UNESCO mud temples and intact millenarian bodhisattva frescoes.

Dhankar Razor Cliff Fort Tabo 996 AD Frescoes Pin Valley Crimson Canyons

What to Eat in Spiti Valley

Traditional trans-Himalayan Tibetan recipes engineered for mountain stamina.

🍞

Spitian Tingmo & Shaptak

Soft steamed floral yeast bread rolls paired with fiery stir-fried mountain vegetables, paneer, or spiced mutton shaptak.

🍜

Hand-Pulled Thentuk

Flat bite-sized hand-pulled noodles simmered in aromatic broth of garlic, mountain radishes, ginger, and local leafy greens.

🥣

Tsampa & Yak Butter Tea

Roasted Himalayan barley flour kneaded with salty churned yak butter tea (Po Cha). The ultimate endurance food of high lamas.

🫐

Wild Seabuckthorn Tea

Brewed from wild golden-orange thorny berries gathered on Spiti riverbeds, bursting with high vitamin C and oxygen boosters.

High Altitude Safety

Guidelines for Spiti Valley Expeditions

  • ✓ Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS): Acclimatize at lower altitudes (Kalpa or Narkanda) before sleeping in Kaza. Drink 4-5 liters of water daily; avoid rapid ascents.
  • ✓ Cash & Connectivity: ATMs in Kaza frequently run dry and digital payments often fail due to optical fiber cuts. Carry sufficient cash from Shimla or Manali.
  • ✓ Permits & Eco-Sanctity: Foreign nationals require Inner Line Permits (ILP) obtained via Reckong Peo or Kaza. Leave zero plastic and do not take ancient fossils out of Langza.
