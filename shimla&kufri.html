Colonial Elegance of Shimla & Alpine Slopes of Kufri

Spanning seven historic ridgelines swathed in oak and rhododendron, Shimla was the summer capital of British India. Today, its grand pedestrian promenade, Victorian Gothic architecture, and the UNESCO Kalka-Shimla Toy Train evoke unmatched nostalgia. Just 16 kilometers up the ridge lies Kufri—a snowy playground offering panoramic vistas of the greater Himalayan snowline.

Must-Visit Sights in Shimla

Iconic colonial ridgelines, sacred summits, and imperial architectural landmarks.

1. The Ridge & Christ Church

Neo-Gothic Heart of Shimla

The grand open plaza at the crest of town. Christ Church features five stained glass windows representing Faith, Hope, Charity, Fortitude, and Patience, and the historic Tudor library building.

Location: Town Center Panoramic Snowline Views

2. Jakhoo Temple & Ropeway

Colossal 108-Foot Hanuman Statue

Shimla's highest peak, where Lord Hanuman is said to have rested while searching for the Sanjeevani herb. The world's tallest Hanuman statue towers above dense deodars, accessible by thrilling aerial ropeway.

Ropeway: 6 mins from Ridge Highest Point in Town

3. Viceregal Lodge (IIAS)

Scottish Baronial Architecture

Perched on Observatory Hill, this imposing stone palace was the summer residence of British Viceroys and witnessed the historic 1945 Shimla Conference. Now houses the Indian Institute of Advanced Study.

Location: Observatory Hill (3 km) Imperial Heritage & Gardens

4. Mall Road & Gaiety Theatre

1887 Victorian Opera House

The bustling colonial street lined with antique lamp posts, Lakkar Bazaar wooden handicrafts, and the neo-Gothic Gaiety Theatre where Rudyard Kipling and Lord Baden-Powell once performed.

Shopping: Wooden toys & Walking sticks Tudor Heritage

5. Annandale & Army Heritage

Historic Polo Ground & Military Relics

A serene flat meadow nestled in a deep pine bowl. Used since the 1830s for polo, picnics, and cricket matches. Today it hosts the prestigious Army Heritage Museum and lush golf putting greens.

Distance: 4 km from Ridge Military Heritage

6. Chadwick Falls & Summer Hill

Cascading Waters in Glen Forest

Tucked inside the deep pine forest of the Glen, the falls drop steeply down an 86-meter gorge. Nearby Summer Hill features quaint university cottages where Mahatma Gandhi stayed during his visits.

Hike: 45 min woodland walk Quiet Forest Retreat

Wonders of Kufri, Chail & Mashobra

Winter ski slopes, wildlife sanctuaries, and royal summer retreats across high pine ridges.

1. Mahasu Peak & Kufri Slopes

Winter Tobogganing & Ski Bowl

The highest point in Kufri. Accessible by foot or horseback through dense cedar woods. On clear winter days, it rewards visitors with a stunning panorama of Badrinath and Kedarnath snow ranges.

Trail: 2 km horse trail Badrinath Panorama

2. Himalayan Nature Park

High Altitude Fauna Sanctuary

Sprawled across 90 hectares of alpine forest, this park protects the rare Western Tragopan, Himalayan Monal (state bird), Tibetan wolf, musk deer, and the elusive snow leopard.

Location: Kufri Center Himalayan Monal & Musk Deer

3. Fagu Valley & Apple Belt

Misty Terraces Overlooking Giri Basin

Just 6 km beyond Kufri, Fagu is an enchanting village surrounded by terraced apple orchards and enveloped in swirling mountain mist. Offers peaceful trails and views of the Giri River valley.

Distance: 22 km from Shimla Foggy Terraces & Homestays

4. Chail Palace & Cricket Ground

Maharaja of Patiala's Summer Seat

Built in 1891 by Maharaja Bhupinder Singh, Chail boasts the world's highest cricket ground at 2,444 meters, ringed by giant chir pines, and a palatial sandstone residence set in 75 acres of royal lawns.

Drive: 45 km from Shimla World's Highest Cricket Pitch

5. Mashobra & Craignano Park

Italian Villa & Cypress Walkways

A serene town known for dense cedar groves and Craignano Nature Park—built in 1890 in Italian architectural style with stone benches, brass water conduits, and the Presidential summer retreat (The Retreat).

Distance: 10 km from Shimla Presidential Retreat
\

6. Naldehra 9-Hole Golf Glade

Lord Curzon's Historic Cedar Links

Established in 1905 by Viceroy Lord Curzon, who was so captivated by the rolling cedar glade that he named his daughter Alexandra Naldehra. Features one of India's oldest and most scenic 9-hole golf courses.

Distance: 22 km from Shimla Oldest Golf Glade in India
Handcrafted Journey

The Ideal 3-Day Shimla & Kufri Itinerary

Carefully structured for grand colonial sights, pine glades, and alpine snow adventures.

DAY 1

Toy Train Arrival, The Ridge Promenade & Mall Road Cafes

Arrive in Shimla (preferably via the heritage Kalka-Shimla Toy Train over historic stone viaducts). Take the municipal lift up to the Mall Road. Explore the pedestrian plaza of The Ridge, admire Christ Church’s stained glass, and browse Lakkar Bazaar for wooden souvenirs. Savor hot coffee and warm plum cake at a heritage cafe while watching the sunset over the valley.

The Ridge & Christ Church Lakkar Bazaar Crafts Mall Road Heritage Cafe
DAY 2

Jakhoo Peak Ropeway & Viceregal Lodge Imperial Heritage

Board the Jakhoo Ropeway from the Ridge to fly over pine treetops up to the 2,455m summit of Jakhoo Hill. Seek blessings at the Hanuman temple beneath the 108-foot statue. In the afternoon, visit the magnificent Scottish Baronial Viceregal Lodge (IIAS) on Observatory Hill and stroll through Annandale's lush polo grounds and Army Museum.

Jakhoo Aerial Ropeway Viceregal Lodge Tour Annandale Army Heritage
DAY 3

Kufri Snow Glade, Fagu Apple Terraces & Mashobra Woods

Drive 16 km up the ridge to Kufri. Ride or hike up Mahasu Peak for snow views across Badrinath and Kedarnath peaks. Visit the rare Himalayan Monal and snow leopards at the Nature Park. Continue 6 km to Fagu village for mist-covered apple orchard views. Return via Mashobra and Craignano Nature Park for a peaceful walk under giant cypress trees.

Mahasu Peak Adventure Himalayan Nature Park Craignano Italian Park

Travel Smart

Guidelines for Shimla & Kufri Trips

  • ✓ Mall Road Vehicle Ban: The Mall Road and Ridge are strictly pedestrian zones. Park at the Circular Road multi-level parking and use the HPTDC Lift.
  • ✓ Jakhoo Monkey Alert: Rhesus macaques at Jakhoo are notorious for snatching glasses and bags. Keep sunglasses and food securely tucked in backpacks.
  • ✓ Winter Black Ice: Between December and February, the Kufri-Fagu stretch gets dangerous black ice in the mornings. Drive with snow chains or hire local cabs.
