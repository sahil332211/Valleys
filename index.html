Dalhousie

Beeji's Peak & Dainkund Peak

Spread across five deodar-clad hills, Dalhousie exudes an old-world Victorian serenity. Walk the Subhash Chowk, Gandhi Chowk, trek through misty Bakrota trails, and enjoy crisp mountain air.

Great Vibes
🌲 Fresh Air & Fragrance ☕ Dainkund Peak temple Chai 🔔 Church Bells ❄️ Dainkund Snow ❄️ Dainkund Peak Corn(ਮੱਕੀ (makki))
1,920 Meters Mini Switzerland

Khajjiar

Beautiful & Scenic View , Mini Lake and glaading

Officially recognized by the Swiss Chancellery in 1992. Khajjiar is a magical saucer-shaped green meadow crowned by dense cedar forests with a floating reed island at its center.

Best Vibes
🌱 Natural Vibes 🐎 Horse Riding 🪷 Floating Grass Island 🌲 Kalatop Woods 🌊Mini Lake
